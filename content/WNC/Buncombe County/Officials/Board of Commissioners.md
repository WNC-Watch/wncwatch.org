The Board of Commissioners cannot direct the sheriff, but it adopts his budget every June, certifies his contracts, and co-signs the federal forfeiture paperwork ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]). The last commissioners to question the RTIC on the record, Jasmine Beach-Ferrara and Brownie Newman, left the board in 2024. No statement on the network from any current commissioner was found as of Oct 7, 2026. Two votes are on record. On April 2, 2024, Amanda Edwards, Martin Moore, Terri Wells and Al Whitesides were among the five who approved $250,000 of forfeiture money "to purchase surveillance equipment," 5 to 0, with no discussion recorded ([minutes](https://media.buncombenc.gov/common/commissioners/minutes/2024-04-02.pdf)). On Oct 6, 2026, the board removed a $290,000 forfeiture request for the "FY27 FUSUS/Axon Contract" from its agenda on Drew Ball's motion, seconded by Moore, with no reason stated ([county video, 0:02:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1XbypN6jVfM&t=173s); [[WNC/Buncombe County/index#The Oct 6, 2026 forfeiture request|the request]]).

Every budget that funds the network has passed unanimously, most recently the $484.4M FY2027 budget on June 2, 2026 ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/buncombe-county-approves-4844m-fy-2027-budget-398-cent-property-tax-hike-fiscal-year-inflation-helene-medical-costs-commissioners-community-unified-fire-district-asheville-city-schools)). The roll call on the July 2022 RTIC appropriation is not in any source found; the minutes would have it.

- **District 1:** Anna Stearns (D, Black Mountain attorney; platform lists housing, infrastructure, mental health, addiction services, nothing on public safety per [her site](https://www.annaforbuncombe.com/meet-anna)) vs Rob Stetson (R)

No surveillance position has been found for any of the four contested-seat candidates (as of Aug 22, 2026). The question for each: would they separate surveillance technology into its own line in the sheriff's budget and condition it on published audits? ([[Election 2026]])
