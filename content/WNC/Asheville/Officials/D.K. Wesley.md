**Title:** City Manager, City of Asheville (Dakisha "DK" Wesley)
**In this role since:** January 12, 2026 (appointed unanimously by council December 5, 2025, from more than 50 applicants; previously assistant county manager, Buncombe County, July 2019 to January 2026; before that Lake County, IL, where she oversaw the Law & Judicial portfolio, and 12 years with Fort Worth, TX)
**Current term ends:** — (serves at council's pleasure; not elected)
**Next voter decision:** none directly; the council that hires and fires the city manager is on the ballot **Nov 3, 2026** · [[Election 2026]]

Asheville runs on the council-manager system: council approves decisions and the manager carries them out. For the [[What Is the RTIC|RTIC]], the contract terms and the choice of police chief are the manager's.

## Record on cameras and surveillance

- **May 7, 2026.** Five days before the [[2026-05-12 City Council|RTIC vote]], told council the Axon agreement it was about to authorize "does not exist yet... B2 will give me authorization to further negotiate and finalize that contract with Axon." In the same briefing, when [[Kim Roney|Roney]] noted that the operative Axon documents were hard to find on the city site: "they're public documents, so there's no reason why we cannot ensure access." [[2026-05-07 Agenda Briefing|The briefing record]].
- **May 12, 2026.** Council authorized her to negotiate and sign the 7.5-year [[What Axon Contracts Do|Axon contract]], 6 to 1. The final terms (retention, data ownership, the [[What Axon Contracts Do#The default data program|data program]], termination rights, renewal pricing) are whatever the executed contract says.
- **July 16, 2026.** Emails [[The RTIC Policy|released Aug 20, 2026]] show APD's business manager telling Axon's representative the contract was ready for signature. 
- **August 4, 2026.** Signed City of Asheville Contract 92700065 with Axon, $7,423,161.15 to Dec 31, 2033, which the city posted in August on its Technology and Public Safety page ([[The Axon Contract as Signed|the contract, as signed]]). The itemized quote and the 20-camera Fleet contract are not posted ([[Records Center|request 1]] · [[Promise Tracker|row 12]]).
- **August 25, 2026.** After council terminated the Flock contract, gave the assurance that stands in place of the plate-reader moratorium that failed for lack of a second: "we do have a contract with [Axon]... before any conversation can happen around putting up the stationary fixed location [ALPRs]... absolutely not, we will not put those up until that community conversation happens." Her staff also committed to notify council when the Flock data deletion is complete. [[2026-08-25 City Council|The meeting record]].

The permanent police chief is her appointment. Applications closed July 31, 2026, and the city said a "comprehensive and collaborative search" would produce a name by the end of September 2026 ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/asheville-city-manager-police-department-chief-retirement-interim-jackie-stepp-community-dk-wesley-public-safety-coalition-trust-september-needs-statement-turnover-decade-culture-mike-lamb-david-zack-tammy-hooper)). The chief inherits the RTIC, [[Promise Tracker|the commitments APD made]], and [[The RTIC Policy|Policy 1703]], which can be revised without a council vote.

Her Buncombe County service, July 2019 to January 2026, overlapped the period in which the sheriff's office built its Fusus camera network ([[WNC/Buncombe County/index|Buncombe County]]). Which county departments she oversaw was not published, and no public record connects her county role to the sheriff's office.
