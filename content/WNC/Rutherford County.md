## The vandalized camera: what the viral reaction shows and doesn't

Two cautions about the July 2026 comment flood: that volume on a 4,000-person town's page is **national virality, not local sentiment**; treat it as evidence of nothing else, in either direction, including the possibility of inauthentic amplification (unverifiable from outside). And per [[How Cities Won]], vandalism-celebration content *helps the surveillance side* regardless of who posts it.

## The inventory

- **Rutherfordton PD** (Chief Clint Ingle): active Flock cameras (~$3,500 each to replace); count unpublished.
- **Forest City PD**: **14 Flock cameras, including audio detection, plus a private-camera registry.** The audio capability matters legally: North Carolina's wiretap statute (G.S. 15A-287) makes intercepting conversations you're not party to a **felony**, and microphones on public streets record conversations no one consented to. Nobody in county government has addressed this on the record. ([[Before You Plug In]] on private-camera integration)
- **Sheriff's Office**: see the timeline: the February 2025 pitch died without a vote, and the county-owned count remains a records-request target.

## The paperwork

Rutherford has no 287(g) agreement with ICE ([ICE list, Aug 21, 2026](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/about/offices/ero/287g/participatingAgencies08212026.xlsx)) and no published sheriff's policy manual. The Sheriff's Office, Rutherfordton PD, and Forest City PD all appear on [[Henderson County]]'s list of 2,048 agencies granted access to Henderson's plate reads (Aug 22, 2026), which is the "nationwide database" the February 2025 pitch was selling. Federal forfeiture payments to the Sheriff's Office: $9,816 (FY2017), $16,834 (FY2020), $53,944 (FY2021), $76,021 (FY2022) ([DOJ](https://www.justice.gov/afp/fy2024-equitable-sharing-payment-cash-and-sales-proceeds-recipient-agency-state)). What the commission can do about a sheriff's cameras: [[What the Law Lets the County Do]].
