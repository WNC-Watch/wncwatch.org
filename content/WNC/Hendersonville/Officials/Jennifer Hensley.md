**Title:** Mayor Pro Tem and Council Member, City of Hendersonville ([the city's council page](https://www.hendersonvillenc.gov/mayor-city-council/contact-city-council-members))
**In this role since:** elected to council in November 2019, re-elected in 2024; mayor pro tem ([the city's council page](https://www.hendersonvillenc.gov/mayor-city-council/contact-city-council-members))
**Next voter decision:** the November 3, 2026 mayoral race, against former Mayor Pro Tem Jerry Smith Jr. ([Hendersonville Lightning, Dec 19, 2025](https://www.hendersonvillelightning.com/four-seasons-politics/15729-elx-4.html))

## Surveillance record

- **Sept 3, 2026.** At Police Chief [[Blair Myhand]]'s presentation on the city's fifteen plate readers ([[WNC/Hendersonville/index#What the chief told council, Sept 3|the full account, with timestamps]]), she asked council's questions: how many documented cases of misuse there had been since the cameras went up in 2024 ([1:06:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvEHZ6zMyBk&t=4008s); the answer was none); how another agency requests access to the city's cameras, after a meeting she described with the county's plate-reader technician ([1:09:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvEHZ6zMyBk&t=4140s)); how officers stalked partners before this technology existed ([1:10:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvEHZ6zMyBk&t=4217s)); whether any device the department owns can trace a phone's signal ([1:12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvEHZ6zMyBk&t=4331s); the answer was no); and whether a public records request had gone unanswered ([1:12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uvEHZ6zMyBk&t=4376s)). On the recording she asked questions and did not state a position on the cameras. The item ended without a vote or any direction to staff.

## The question for her

As a candidate for mayor: would she bring the Flock contract, the department's sharing settings, and the retention period to a council vote? The Sept 3 item carried no motion, and the contract was not posted as of Sept 8.
