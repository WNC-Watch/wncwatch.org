Every entry below happened, is dated, and links to reporting or a primary document you can read. Nothing here is a prediction. The categories follow the questions people actually ask: can it reach federal agencies, can it follow someone to a clinic, can an officer use it on an ex, can it stop the wrong person, can it be hidden from a defendant, can it leak, and what do the companies do when a town says no. Entries marked **NC** happened in North Carolina. Corrections welcome; this page is meant to be checked.

## How to use this page

Pick the category that matches the claim you are answering. "It can't be used for immigration" has four dated answers. "Officers are audited" has the Mooresville outcome: thirty-one searches, found by a monthly audit, after the fact. "The cameras are only for serious crime" has a phone ticket and a pretext stop. The point is not that every one of these will happen in Asheville; it is that every one of them happened somewhere that was told it would not.
