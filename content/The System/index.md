This section explains the machine: what the RTIC is, what the cameras collect, what the data turns into, and who lives under it first.

- **[[What Is the RTIC]]**: Plain-language explainer on what was actually approved: the center, the cameras, the sharing radius, the retention, the vendors.
- **[[What They Can Actually Learn About You]]**: What a plate scan becomes: your routes, your associations, your name, from court records, audit logs, and APD's own briefings.
- **[[Who Gets Watched]]**: The lived stakes for immigrant families, people traveling here for reproductive care, and protesters. The promises made to them failed everywhere audits were pulled.
- **[[Watching the Poor]]**: About 500 cameras already watch Asheville's housing communities. The national record shows what that becomes.
- **[[Sheriffs Audit Log|The Sheriff's Audit Log]]**: Twenty-eight months of the county's own Fusus access records: 58 percent of camera viewing on public housing, almost all of it live, most of it by three accounts, every timestamp withheld.
- **[[Follow the Money]]**: The BID, the TDA, the earmark, and the forfeiture funds: who pays for the buildout and who asked for it.
- **[[Before You Plug In|Before You Connect Your Cameras]]**: For business owners: what integrating your cameras with the police network actually signs you up for.
