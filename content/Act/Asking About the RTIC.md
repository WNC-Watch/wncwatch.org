Every ask on this page names who can answer it and the document it rests on, so an answer or a silence goes on the record. The facts are on [[The Real-Time Intelligence Center]], [[The Axon Contract as Signed|the contract, as signed]], and [[The Technology and Public Safety Page|the city's page, annotated]]; the Flock exit has its own list on [[Getting Flock Out]]; comment procedure is on [[Speak at Council]].

## What was read

The city's Technology and Public Safety page as of Sept 23, 2026; City of Asheville Contract 92700065 with Axon, the eight pages the city posted; the FUSUS appendix; the Flock contract with Flock's terms attached; both plate-reader portals; the Aug 25, 2026 minutes; APD's March 2026 committee slides; the Sept 24, 2026 committee agenda.

## What the city has done

The city posted the executed Axon contract, which residents had asked for since August. Both plate-reader portals are live, and the Axon portal publishes retention, device counts and hotlist categories. The page states that APD's Flock accounts are deactivated. The page invites questions and says it will revise its answers. The city attorney's office caught Axon's default data program and opted out. Each of those is a step the record shows.

## What the record shows and does not show

1. The contract references an itemized quote (Q-808220-46198AS) that is not posted. Products, quantities and the Fusus tier are in it. Kim Roney asked for "Axon's detailed purchasing lists" on Aug 25.
2. The contract's scope lists "Lightpost," Axon's fixed plate reader, in APD's current inventory. The mayor said Aug 25 the city has none. The portal shows none active. No document explains the line.
3. The only barrier to fixed Axon readers is the city manager's verbal assurance of Aug 25. A moratorium motion that night died for lack of a second.
4. Policy 1703 is not posted; its effective date was blank in the last released draft; the page says it will be published. The policy sets no retention period, no user cap, no publication duty, and does not mention immigration.
5. No audit has been published. The first quarterly window under the policy closes about October 2026; the Sept 24 committee agenda has no audit item. The July 2025 audit-log request was refused.
6. The page says 30-day retention four times; the chief, the memo and both portals say 21 days.
7. The page describes camera registration (Level 1) and omits integration (Level 2, a CORE device giving live feed access), which APD plans to solicit this fall.
8. The city's engagement is scheduled for "late 2026 or early 2027." Fusus billing starts Dec 1, 2026; integration solicitation is planned for fall 2026; full operation is projected for spring 2027. The ten-item review ordered Aug 25 has no published schedule or reviewer.
9. The 7.5-year cost is $7,423,161.15, of which $6,955,559 is city money, rising about 5 percent a year through year 6, against the $30,000 a year the city paid before for Fusus access. The mayor described the grant in May as a way to "save the city money."
10. The separate contract for 20 Axon Fleet in-car readers, described in July 2026 emails, is not posted and has never been presented to council.
11. Axon's written confirmation of the ACEIP opt-out, which Axon's appendix says it provides within 30 days, is not posted.

## At the podium, three minutes

Council comment is three minutes per speaker; the committee's comment period is 30 minutes total. The procedure and the sign-up rules are on [[Speak at Council]].

1. Who you are, 15 seconds: name, neighborhood, one line of stake.
2. What you read, 30 seconds: "I read the city's new Technology and Public Safety page and the Axon contract the city posted there, all eight pages, and the Axon transparency portal."
3. What the city did, 15 seconds: "Thank you for posting the contract and for the portals. That is what residents asked for in August."
4. One fact, 45 seconds. Pick one:
   - "The contract lists Lightpost, Axon's fixed plate reader, as part of APD's current inventory. The mayor said on Aug 25 the city has none. I would like the manager to say in writing what that line means."
   - "The contract's schedule runs from $740,000 this year to $947,211 in year six, and $6.96 million of the $7.42 million is city money. Before the RTIC the city paid $30,000 a year for Fusus access."
   - "The page says the community will be engaged in late 2026 or early 2027. APD's own notes say it will solicit camera integrations this fall and open the center in spring 2027. The billing for Fusus starts December 1."
   - "The page says every search requires a case number and a quarterly audit. The city refused to release the audit log in September 2025, and no audit has been published. The first one under the new policy is due about October."
5. One ask, 45 seconds, with the owner named: "I ask the city manager to post the itemized Axon quote the contract references, and to put her Aug 25 assurance in writing: no fixed Axon reader without a council vote."
6. The alternative, 15 seconds: "The ABC board's closed camera system, raised at this table on Aug 25, needs a subpoena to open. That conversation has not been had."
7. The commitment, 15 seconds: "I will be back at the next meeting for the answer."
