**Who:** a 30-year-old Edneyville berry farmer and beekeeper; founder of DeFlock Henderson County
**Follow:** [the defund petition](https://www.change.org/p/stop-funding-mass-surveillance-defund-flock-cameras-in-henderson-county-nc) · the DeFlock Henderson County Facebook page

## What he has done

Rhodes addressed the Henderson County commissioners on August 3, 2026, urging them not to renew Flock funding. When the board gave no response, he started a Facebook group and a petition that gathered 880 signatures in under three days and **2,071 by August 17** ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/henderson-county-flock-camera-petition-privacy-concerns-license-plate-readers-sheriffs-office-lowell-griffin) · [Hendersonville Lightning](https://www.hendersonvillelightning.com/news/16237-surveillance-cameras-trigger-deflock-henderson-county-a-citizen-uprising.html) · [[Henderson County]]). On Flock's safeguards: "unwise and foolish to use the honor system." He has also argued the camera money could go to Helene recovery instead.

## Why it matters

He is a first-time, single-issue organizer in a county whose chair says he sees no problem "until we hear of some abuse" ([[Bill Lapsley]]). The petition's growth curve is the only pressure the board has acknowledged. The precedent one ridge west, [[Macon County]], was driven by exactly this kind of constituent volume.
