## What he has done

Coggins films Macon County commission meetings and Franklin town board meetings, the latter of which have no official video at all, and runs a companion weather and public-safety service. His footage is free and donation-supported, with no newsroom behind it; for the July 14, 2026 de-Flock vote the county's own channel carries the official record and his coverage fills the gaps ([[Macon County]]).
