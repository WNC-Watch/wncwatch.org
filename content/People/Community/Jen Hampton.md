## What she has done

On April 14, 2026, after more than 40 residents arrived to speak and the $1.14M item was pulled from the agenda, Hampton spoke anyway: "Let's call it what it actually is: a centralized hub for spying on the people of Asheville." ([2:25:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QHT2NetBNmI&t=8752s) · [[Timeline]] · [[The People Who Showed Up]]) The coalition she organizes with opposed the downtown Business Improvement District in 2024 and the RTIC in 2026, the same people on both ([[Follow the Money]]). Service workers' late-night safety was, by the commission chair's own account, the original justification for the county's downtown patrol program ([[Buncombe County]]); the workers' organization turned out to be the opposition.
