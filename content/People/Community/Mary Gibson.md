**Who:** a Madison County resident (relatively recent arrival, with her family) who began attending monthly commission meetings and organizes with [Madison for Privacy](https://www.madisonforprivacy.org/)
**Follow:** [Madison for Privacy](https://www.madisonforprivacy.org/) (madisonforprivacy@proton.me)

## What she has done

Gibson organized "Popsicles for Privacy" during the August 2026 national week of action, an information session deliberately designed to "give the impression of having a friendly discussion about why you should care about this," the opposite register from the June 9 meeting where Chairman [[Michael Garrison]] told residents "You will not speak on Flock tonight" ([Atlanta Press Collective](https://atlpresscollective.com/2026/08/15/flock-week-of-action/) · [[Madison County]]). Her formal role within Madison for Privacy is not published; she is documented as the organizer of this event.
