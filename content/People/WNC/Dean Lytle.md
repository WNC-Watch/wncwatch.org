## Surveillance record

Lytle called Brevard's plate-reader proposal a "hot button" issue and, through the Public Safety Committee, pushed for a public input meeting before any council vote on what became an Axon bundle of three "free" cameras inside an ~$80,000 five-year package ([Transylvania Times](https://www.transylvaniatimes.com/news/public-input-sought-before-lpr-decision/article_c8ec9c34-eafd-49aa-9777-0f1590911bdd.html) · [[Transylvania County]] · [[What Axon Contracts Do]]). The meeting remains unscheduled; the committee has sent the question back for input at least three times.

## The question for him

A date. The public-input meeting is the best intervention point in the region and it does not exist until it is on a calendar.
