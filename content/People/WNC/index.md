description: The sheriffs, commissioners, and police chiefs who decide the cameras in the counties around Asheville, county by county, with the date voters next get a say.

Every county around Asheville has its own camera decision and its own deciders. Sheriffs run the systems and answer only to voters; commissioners fund them; chiefs run the town networks. Profiles below; the county pages carry the full record. What each office can legally do: [[What the Law Lets the County Do]].
