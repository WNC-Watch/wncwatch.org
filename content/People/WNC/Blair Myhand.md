**Title:** Chief of Police, City of Hendersonville

## Surveillance record

Myhand's department runs 16 cameras (15 plate readers) at about $45,000 a year; its Flock portal on August 22, 2026 showed 373,294 unique vehicles in 30 days, 83 searches, 4,703 hot-list hits, and 304 alerts, three from the NCIC "Gang or Suspected Terrorist" file ([portal](https://transparency.flocksafety.com/hendersonville-nc-pd) · [[Henderson County]]). His summary of the technology: "ALPRs are the best law enforcement technology since the two-way radio." Unlike the county sheriff's portal, the city's does not display which agencies can access its data.

## The question for him

Why does Hendersonville's portal omit the sharing list that the sheriff's publishes? The radio did not send every conversation to 2,048 agencies.
