**Current term ends:** December 2026
**Next voter decision:** **Nov 3, 2026** vs Republican [[Robin Lyles]], a rematch of 2022 (Harwood 68.8% to 31.2%); each was the sole filer in his party's primary ([Madison County BOE candidate list](https://www.madisoncountync.gov/uploads/5/9/7/0/59701963/2026_candidate_list.pdf) · [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Buddy_Harwood_(Madison_County_Sheriff,_North_Carolina,_candidate_2022))) · [[Election 2026]]

## Surveillance record

By March 2026 his office had **15 Flock cameras** in use countywide, announced via the sheriff's Facebook page, with no commission vote and no public discussion; published audit logs show 1,200+ nationwide-database searches in 60 days and 3,302 searches across 33 offense categories in five months, in a county of about 20,000 people. The office has **refused the bulk of public records requests** for the contract, expenditures, audits, and policies ([[Madison County]] · [Madison for Privacy](https://www.madisonforprivacy.org/)). No transparency portal exists; the office does appear on [[Henderson County]]'s list of agencies with access to Henderson's reads.

The records refusals are themselves a record: state law makes the written ALPR policy and the annual audit report documents that must exist ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]), and a sheriff who will not produce them is either out of compliance or hiding compliant documents.
