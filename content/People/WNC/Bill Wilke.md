**Title:** Haywood County Sheriff · Republican (the county's first Republican sheriff in about a century)
**In this role since:** December 2022 (elected November 2022 with about 61% over Democrat Larry Bryson)
**Current term ends:** December 2026
**Next voter decision:** none: lost the March 3, 2026 Republican primary to [[Mark Mease]], 42.2% to 57.8% ([Smoky Mountain News](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/40976-mease-defeats-incumbent-wilke-in-haywood-sheriff-primary))

## Surveillance record

Wilke's office put roughly eight to ten Flock plate readers into use in 2024 through ordinary operations, with no commission vote then or since ([[Haywood County]]). No on-record statement by Wilke about the cameras, their data sharing, or ICE access was found in this pass; the January 2026 candidate forum coverage did not touch the subject ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/haywood-county-sheriff-forum-wilke-mease-howell-2026-smoky-mountain-news-primary-election-day)). The cameras he installed outlast him; the contract and renewal date are unpublished.

## Background

Thirty-one years in the Army, Army Reserve, and National Guard (retired as a colonel in 2021); sixteen years at the Asheville Police Department, to lieutenant; private investigator from 2016 ([The Mountaineer](https://www.themountaineer.com/news/haywood-county-will-soon-have-a-new-sheriff-in-town)). Named with a deputy in a 2025 civil suit over a landfill altercation; the landfill countersued ([Smoky Mountain News](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/40035-man-who-shot-viral-video-at-local-dump-sues-haywood-sheriff-dump-owner)).

## The question for him

Before he leaves: what did the Flock contract cost, when does it renew, and who has access to the data? He can answer without an election.
