**Title:** Chief of Police, Town of Rutherfordton (with the department since 2005; chief since May 1, 2020) ([Rutherford Weekly](https://www.rutherfordweekly.com/clint-ingle-is-rutherfordton-s-new-police-chief-cms-10046))

## Surveillance record

Ingle's consistent public line is that the cameras are for serious crimes, missing persons, and alerts, not traffic enforcement. After a Flock camera was found damaged in July 2026 he put the replacement cost at about $3,500 and the department's statement promised anyone responsible "will be arrested when appropriate"; as of August 22 no arrest has been reported ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/rutherfordton-police-address-issue-of-damaged-flock-cameras-rutherfordton-chief-rutherford-county-amber-alert-silver-identify-suspects-public-safety) · [Annie Dance](https://anniedance.substack.com/p/rutherfordton-police-defend-flock) · [[Rutherford County]]). The 24,000-plus comments were national virality, not local sentiment, and vandalism hands the surveillance side its best argument ([[How Cities Won]]).

## The question for him

How many cameras does the town run, who can search them, and has the department ever audited a search? The count has never been published.
