## Why his silence matters

Haywood's cameras entered with no vote and were kept in July 2026 on a chairman's statement, not a vote ([[Haywood County]]). Whoever wins in November decides, alone, whether the contract renews, who the data is shared with, and whether the office's jail cooperates with ICE beyond what HB 318 requires ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]). Nobody has asked him.

## The question for him

Would he keep the Flock contract, publish the sharing list and the annual audit, and put the renewal before the commission for a vote?
