## Surveillance record

A week after [[Macon County]] removed its cameras, Ensley told WLOS that Haywood would keep its 8 to 10: overdose deaths had fallen "in the thirties... last year we only had ten," and the county had received "no complaints" ([WLOS, July 21, 2026](https://wlos.com/news/local/haywood-county-keep-flock-safety-cameras-macon-county-removes-system-ai-powered-camera-license-plate-reader-automatic-artificial-intelligence-western-north-carolina)). No vote accompanied the decision, just as none accompanied the 2024 deployment. Overdose deaths fell statewide and nationally over the same period; no analysis connects Haywood's plate readers to its numbers ([[Haywood County]]).

## The question for him

If the cameras are worth keeping, are they worth a vote, a published contract, and a sharing list? "No complaints" is not oversight ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]).
