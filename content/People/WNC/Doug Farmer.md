**Title:** Jackson County Sheriff · Republican
**In this role since:** December 2022 (elected 2022 over Democrat Rick Buchanan, 51.9% to 48.1%; previously Macon County Sheriff's Office from 1998, Highlands PD, a police-liaison contract in Iraq in 2006, and Sylva PD until 2019)
**Current term ends:** December 2030 (re-elected)
**Next voter decision:** none until 2030: won the March 3, 2026 Republican primary over Brandon Elders with over 72%; no Democrat filed ([Smoky Mountain News](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/item/40973-jackson-county-reelects-farmer-as-sheriff) · [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Doug_Farmer_(Jackson_County_Sheriff,_North_Carolina,_candidate_2026)))

## Surveillance record

Farmer's office took a **5-year, $55,000+ Flock contract** for 4 cameras in 2024; one month of the county's own records (June 30 to July 30, 2026) shows **1,190,043 vehicle reads, 563 searches, 4,652 hot-list hits**, data shared with 280 in-state agencies, and 17 of 82 employees with access. At the August 4, 2026 work session he called the system a "force multiplier" with zero documented misuse; commissioners raised concerns; no vote was taken ([[Jackson County]] · [Sylva Herald](https://www.thesylvaherald.com/top_stories/article_67b7d742-f656-4a21-b404-5eb15ffebbfa.html) · [Smoky Mountain News](https://smokymountainnews.com/news/news-latest/jackson-county-talks-flock/)).

His office's public [policy manual](https://public.powerdms.com/jcso/documents) contains four documents and no plate-reader policy, which state law requires in writing; the one technology policy it does publish is an unedited Colorado template referring to "the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office" ([[Jackson County]]).

## ICE

Honors detainers case by case: "When we arrest somebody and we have charges on them, we'll send that information up to see if ICE wants to detain them for any reason" ([Sylva Herald](https://www.thesylvaherald.com/news/article_bbff1653-9aef-451a-83f9-5769df186fec.html), date unconfirmed). No 287(g) agreement ([ICE list, Aug 21, 2026](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/about/offices/ero/287g/participatingAgencies08212026.xlsx)).

## The question for him

Where is the written ALPR policy G.S. 20-183.31 requires, and where is the annual audit? "Zero documented misuse" means nothing if nothing is documented ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]).
