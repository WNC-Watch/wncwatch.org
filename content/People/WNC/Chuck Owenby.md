## Surveillance record

Owenby began installing **8 Flock cameras** in August 2024, on private property near county lines, with no commission vote found ([Transylvania Times](https://www.transylvaniatimes.com/news/license-plate-readers-now-watching-highway-traffic/article_cb1ba808-5be4-11ef-8520-ab798f7d6f7b.html)). He credits them with locating six missing persons and a "reduction of crime"; no independent evaluation exists, no transparency portal is published, and no policy manual is public ([[Transylvania County]]). His office appears on [[Henderson County]]'s list of agencies with access to Henderson's reads. An independent critic site (chuckowenby.com) opposes the program; it is opinion, not reporting.

## The question for him

With no election to answer to for four years, the only accountability left is paper: the contract, the annual audit state law requires, and the sharing list. Will he publish them?
