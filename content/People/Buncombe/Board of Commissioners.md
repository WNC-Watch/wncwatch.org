The Board of Commissioners cannot direct the sheriff, but it adopts his budget every June, certifies his contracts, and co-signs the federal forfeiture paperwork ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]). The last commissioners to question the RTIC on the record, Jasmine Beach-Ferrara and Brownie Newman, left the board in 2024. The current seven have said nothing about it that this record can find.

Every budget that funds the network has passed unanimously, most recently the $484.4M FY2027 budget on June 2, 2026 ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/buncombe-county-approves-4844m-fy-2027-budget-398-cent-property-tax-hike-fiscal-year-inflation-helene-medical-costs-commissioners-community-unified-fire-district-asheville-city-schools)). The roll call on the July 2022 RTIC appropriation is not in any source found; the minutes would have it.

## On the November 3, 2026 ballot

- **District 1:** Anna Stearns (D, Black Mountain attorney; platform lists housing, infrastructure, mental health, addiction services, nothing on public safety per [her site](https://www.annaforbuncombe.com/meet-anna)) vs Rob Stetson (R)
- **District 2 (open):** Lonnie Israel (D; won the primary 57.7% over Terry Threadwell) vs Greg Parks (R)
- **District 3:** Al Whitesides (D), unopposed

No surveillance position has been found for any of the four contested-seat candidates (as of Aug 22, 2026). The question for each: would they separate surveillance technology into its own line in the sheriff's budget and condition it on published audits? ([[Election 2026]])
