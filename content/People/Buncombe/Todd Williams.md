**Title:** District Attorney, Prosecutorial District 40 (Buncombe County) · Democrat
**In this role since:** January 2015 (elected 2014)
**Term ends:** December 31, 2026; not seeking re-election. [[Martin Moore]] won the Democratic primary and has no Republican opponent.

## Surveillance record

Williams wrote a letter supporting the RTIC dated April 30, 2025 (the site's May 12 reconstruction has the date), which Interim Chief [[Jackie Stepp]] read into the record at the [[2026-05-12 City Council|May 12, 2026]] vote alongside U.S. Attorney Russ Ferguson's letter dated the day of the vote. Its operative line: "I have been **assured** that this system cannot be used for that purpose," which makes the county's chief prosecutor's support contingent on someone else's assurance about a system whose contract did not yet exist ([[Jackie Stepp]] · [[Their Claims vs The Record]]). The site's records request 6 asks for both letters in full ([[Records Center]]).

He has also defended plate readers at length on local radio in August 2026; that material is being reviewed before anything from it is published here.
