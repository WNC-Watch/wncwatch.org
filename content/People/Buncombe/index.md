The county built its network first, in 2022, and the city copied its rulebook. These are the people who decide what the county's cameras do: the sheriff (on the ballot), the board that funds him (three seats on the ballot), the staff who sign the checks and the forfeiture paperwork, the school board, and the district attorney who prosecutes with it.
