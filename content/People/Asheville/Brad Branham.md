**Next voter decision:** none; appointed by council

## Surveillance record

Before the May 12, 2026 vote, council was told staff and counsel would provide the contracts and a legal analysis; the Axon contract "did not exist yet" on May 7 and the legal opinion has never been published ([[2026-05-07 Agenda Briefing]] · [[Records Center|request 5]]). No statement by Branham on the RTIC, data sharing, or the anti-sanctuary statutes' effect on APD's no-ICE-sharing promises has been found. The question of whether a city ordinance restricting ALPR data sharing would survive [G.S. 160A-205.2](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/PDF/BySection/Chapter_160A/GS_160A-205.2.pdf) is one his office would have to answer ([[What the Law Lets the County Do]]).
