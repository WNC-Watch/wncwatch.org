## Why NC-11 is on this site

The [[What Is the RTIC|RTIC]] exists because of a congressional earmark: [[Chuck Edwards|Rep. Chuck Edwards]] secured the $1.14M in Community Project Funding after city staff requested it in 2024 without council's knowledge. Edwards **withdrew from the race August 5, 2026** following a House Ethics Committee report ([WLOS](https://wlos.com/news/local/chuck-edwards-withdraws-nc-11-congressional-district-race-jamie-ager-election-impact-midterm)). His successor will hold the earmark pen, and votes on federal surveillance funding streams generally.

## The Edwards vacancy

[[Chuck Edwards|Edwards]] won the March GOP primary but withdrew August 5 after the House Ethics Committee found "substantial reason to believe" he violated House rules on sexual harassment and hostile workplaces, recommending censure ([NC Newsline](https://ncnewsline.com/2026/08/05/republicans-democrats-agree-rep-edwards-made-right-call-withdrawing-from-nc-11-race/)). He says he will serve out his term through January 2027. Ager's November opponent is therefore not Edwards but **[[Jennifer Balkcom]]**, the NC state representative the NC-11 GOP executive committee substituted onto the ballot August 10 ([WUNC](https://www.wunc.org/2026-03-03/democrats-choose-ager-in-nc-11-primary-race-edwards-wins-gop-nod)).
