## August 26, 2026: a briefing statement attributed to the wrong member

The [[2026-08-20 Agenda Briefing]] coverage and [[Maggie Ullman]]'s page credited her with the statement "I was under the impression that 10 were coming down already... I'm happy to move forward on just pulling them now." Voice analysis of the briefing audio, checked against the Aug 25, 2026 session where each member is recognized by the chair, attributes the statement to [[Sage Turner]], whose own public posts describe the same wind-down details. The quote now sits on Turner's page. Ullman's other briefing statement, on not "pulling back on our transparency tools," was re-verified and stands as hers.

## August 26, 2026: the source of the vehicle reader count

The [[2026-08-25 City Council]] page attributed the count of 162 vehicle-mounted Axon plate readers to a staff memo. The source is APD's statement to 828NewsNOW, published Aug 20, 2026; the chief's Aug 14 memo gives no vehicle count. The number is unchanged. The city's Axon transparency portal listed 150 vehicles in February 2025 and 137 on Aug 26, 2026, so APD's stated count and its portal disagree.

## August 24, 2026: two people, two roles

The housing authority's chief executive is Ella Santos; David Nash retired in June 2023, and the page that named him as executive director now says "then-executive director." APD Deputy Chief Sean Aardema sits on the housing authority's board, verified in the board's May 20, 2026 minutes.
